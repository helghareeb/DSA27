# Marking yourself

- **Section A:** one mark per correct letter. The answer tables are at the top of
  each weekly answers file.
- **Section B:** use the **marking points** in the answers files. Award a mark
  for each point your answer makes clearly and correctly — not for length.
- **Then** look at *which* questions you lost marks on, and on which level
  ([what], [how] or [why]). That tells you what to revise, and how.
