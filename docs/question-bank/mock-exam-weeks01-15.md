# Marking yourself

- **Section A:** one mark per correct letter. The answer tables are at the top of
  each weekly answers file.
- **Section B:** use the **marking points** in the answers files. Award a mark
  for each point your answer makes clearly and correctly — not for length. For
  the traces and drawings (B2, B4, B5, B7), divide the marks evenly over the
  steps and award them for the steps you got exactly right.
- **Then** look at *which* questions you lost marks on, and at which level
  ([what], [how] or [why]). That tells you what to revise, and how — and the
  weekly question bank of that week has more of the same kind.
- **Remember the rule that fails students:** you need **≥ 30% of the final** as
  well as ≥ 60% overall. Coursework cannot rescue a final below 30%.
