### Course content, quoted verbatim (p. 138)

> "This course introduces fundamental data structures, algorithms, and abstract
> data types using a mix of programming and theory. Main topics include data
> structures such as arrays, lists, linked lists, stacks, queues, hash tables,

> structures and their operations."

### CS303 — the sequel (p. 144)

`CS303 Analysis and Design of Algorithms`, 3 CH, 2 lecture + 2 lab, prerequisite
**CS201**, Junior Fall, major in CS and AI, minor in NCS, same assessment split
(20/15/5/60). It covers divide-and-conquer, recursion, greedy, **dynamic
programming**, recursion trees, the master method, graph theory, NP-completeness,
sorting and searching lower bounds, minimum spanning trees, shortest paths and
network flows.

**This is why dynamic programming is enrichment in CS201, not examinable
material: the bylaw puts it in CS303.**

### The older course description (2013, 2014 and 2020 bylaws)

The 2013 and 2014 bylaws share one description word for word:

> "Introduce the fundamental concepts of data structures and the algorithms that
> proceed from them. Topics include recursion, the underlying philosophy of
> object-oriented programming, fundamental data structures (including stacks,
> queues, linked lists, hash tables, trees, and graphs), the basics of algorithmic
> analysis, and an introduction to the principles of language translation."

The 2026 description drops "the principles of language translation" and adds
**heaps and priority queues** explicitly. The course got slightly more focused.

## 4. Known contradictions in the source documents

These are real defects in the official PDFs. They are recorded here rather than
silently resolved. **In every case, the 2026 faculty bylaw governs.**

1. **Value of A+.** The 2013 SW Eng. bylaw gives **4.33** on p. 14 but **4.00**
   on p. 57 — inside the same document. The 2014, 2020 and 2026 bylaws all give
   **4.00**. → Use **4.00**.

2. **Final exam weight, "لا تقل" vs "لا تزيد".** The 2013 bylaw p. 13 says the
   final exam mark shall be **not less than** 60%; the 2014 bylaw p. 12 says
   **not more than** 60%. Both documents' own articles on p. 11 say no single
   component may *exceed* 60%, so the 2013 wording appears to be a typo.
   → The 2026 bylaw fixes this: final = **exactly 60**, p. 138.

3. **B− grade band.** Listed under جيد جداً in one table and جيد in another,
   within both the 2013 and 2014 bylaws. → The 2026 scale (p. 22) is the one to
   use.

4. **Two Arabic names for CS2101** in the 2020 AI bylaw — see † above.

5. **Degraded scan.** Pages 41–43, 45–46, 48–50, 53 and 55–58 of the 2020 AI
   bylaw are a poor OCR layer with mangled course codes (`AI3001` printed as
   `A13001`, `UN1001` as `UNIOOI`). Pages 44, 47, 51 and 52 are clean. If you
   need a number from that document, prefer the clean pages or the 2026 bylaw.

6. **Program naming.** The file published as `bio_.pdf` is the **Medical
   Informatics** bylaw, not a Bioinformatics one. See the note at the top.
